You don't have to go through this alone.

If you are going through a very hard time, you are in a safe place. Talking to someone can be the first step, and there are people ready to listen.

Something to hold on to today

What you feel right now is real, but it is not permanent. The darkest moments pass, and many people who thought there was no way out are doing better today.

Small steps

Talk to someone

A friend, a family member or a helpline. You don't need the perfect words.

Move away from anything that could hurt you

If you can, go to a safe place and be near other people.

Reach out to a professional

A therapist or doctor can support you. Asking for help takes courage.
